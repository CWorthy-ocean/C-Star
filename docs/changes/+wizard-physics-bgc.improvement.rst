The blueprint wizard's Forcing card pairs physics and biogeochemistry by stage: one Initial conditions pane and one Boundary forcing pane, each with a Physics and a Biogeochemistry part.
- Settings that apply to the whole initial-conditions or boundary file, such as regridding, validation and flexible start time, sit under a Regridding & roms-tools options link that counts those not at their defaults.
- Pane headers name the BGC sources, and say "check BGC sources" while two or more sources do not split the tracers between them.
- On a model without MARBL, an empty Biogeochemistry part is hidden.
